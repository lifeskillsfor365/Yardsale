About the Campaign

Why We Need This Van

True Love for Special Need Families' adult care center needs a van to bring our participants — adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities — into the community they are a part of. Right now, limited transportation means fewer outings, missed appointments, and fewer opportunities for the people we serve to survive, enjoy, and thrive.

A 15-passenger accessible van will change that. We are raising $18,820 and our Charity Yard Sale is the first big push. Every item sold, every dollar donated, and every person who shows up brings us closer to the goal.
